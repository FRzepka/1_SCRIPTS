\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item A causal benchmark compares four SOC estimators on cell-disjoint LFP data.
  \item Accuracy, robustness, recovery, and embedded cost are evaluated jointly.
  \item The data-driven estimator gives the lowest nominal error and strong robustness.
  \item The ECM observer provides the strongest initialization recovery.
  \item All SOC cores meet the one-second STM32 deadline within available memory.
\end{itemize}
