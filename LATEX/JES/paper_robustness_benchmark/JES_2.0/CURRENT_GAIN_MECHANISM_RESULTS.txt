JES 2.0 - CURRENT-GAIN MECHANISM STUDY
Updated: 2026-08-28

PROTOCOL
  Sensor gain error: I_meas = I * (1 + b).
  Main sweep: b = +/-0.5%, +/-1.5%, and +/-3.0%.
  Reported robustness value: larger Delta-MAE from the matched sign pair.
  Reason: one sign can compensate an estimator's directed baseline error.

COMPLETED TESTS
  1. Natural +/-3% response in all available 24 h cell-state windows.
  2. Five-cell fresh/mid-life/aged comparison (C09, C13, C15, C25, C29).
  3. C29 natural versus disabled full-charge reset (72 runs).
  4. Identical C29 mid-life trace replayed at SOH 0.962, 0.897, and 0.831
     for HDM, HECM, and DD (27 runs).
  5. Continuous 1,344 h C29 trajectory with baseline, +3%, and -3% for all
     four estimators (12 entries; four baseline summaries reused from the
     identical full-C29 pilot protocol).

MAIN RESULTS
  Natural five-cell state means show a mid-life maximum, not a monotonic aging
  increase. The mean absolute cumulative signed charge is also highest in the
  selected mid-life windows.

  Correlation between window Delta-MAE and mean absolute cumulative signed
  charge over 15 cell-state windows:
    DM    r = 0.995
    HDM   r = 0.966
    HECM  r = 0.698
    DD    r = 0.151

  Controlled identical-trace aged/fresh sensitivity ratios:
    HDM   1.103
    HECM  0.971
    DD    0.432
  SOH alone therefore produces only a small amplification for HDM and does not
  explain the natural mid-life peak across all estimators.

  The selected 24 h C29 windows contain no qualifying 300 s CV event. Natural
  and disabled-reset results are consequently identical in those windows.

  The continuous C29 trajectory contains 45 qualifying CV-reset episodes with
  a median inter-reset interval of 29.14 h. Its aged/fresh ratios are:
    DM    1.085
    HDM   1.264
    HECM  1.354
    DD    not defined because both signed perturbations remain below baseline
  For DD, the larger signed-pair Delta-MAE remains negative in fresh, mid-life,
  aged, and full-trajectory summaries. This is reported as a baseline-dependent
  model response, not relabelled as degradation.

INTERPRETATION
  A multiplicative current error integrates with signed charge history between
  correction/reset events. Elapsed time, absolute throughput, and lower SOH do
  not by themselves guarantee a monotonic MAE increase. The primary Figure 05
  can retain the monotonic error-magnitude sweep because it uses the adverse
  direction per cell and magnitude. Figures 05c and 05d provide the mechanism
  and lifecycle evidence needed to prevent an overgeneralized aging claim.

LIMITATIONS
  The controlled and continuous lifecycle tests use C29 as a mechanism study,
  not as six-cell population inference. The five-cell aging comparison excludes
  C27 because C27 has no frozen mid-life or aged window.
