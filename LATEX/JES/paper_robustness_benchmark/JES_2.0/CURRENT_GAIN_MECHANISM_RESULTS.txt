JES 2.0 - CURRENT-GAIN MECHANISM STUDY
Updated: 2026-08-28

PROTOCOL
  Sensor gain error: I_meas = I * (1 + b).
  Main sweep: b = +/-0.5%, +/-1.5%, and +/-3.0%.
  Reported robustness value: larger Delta-MAE from the matched sign pair.
  Reason: one sign can compensate an estimator's directed baseline error.

COMPLETED TESTS
  1. Natural +/-3% response in all available 24 h cell-state windows.
  2. Five-cell fresh/mid-life/aged comparison (C09, C13, C15, C25, C29).
  3. C29 natural versus disabled full-charge reset (72 runs).
  4. Identical C29 mid-life trace replayed at SOH 0.962, 0.897, and 0.831
     for HDM, HECM, and DD (27 runs).
  5. Continuous 1,344 h C29 trajectory with baseline, +3%, and -3% for all
     four estimators (12 entries).
  6. Exact time-resolved matched rerun of all 12 entries with fresh baselines,
     5 min MAE bins, 45 full-charge events, and 44 inter-charge intervals.

MAIN RESULTS
  Natural five-cell state means show a mid-life maximum, not a monotonic aging
  increase. The mean absolute cumulative signed charge is also highest in the
  selected mid-life windows.

  Correlation between window Delta-MAE and mean absolute cumulative signed
  charge over 15 cell-state windows:
    DM    r = 0.995
    HDM   r = 0.966
    HECM  r = 0.698
    DD    r = 0.151

  Controlled identical-trace aged/fresh sensitivity ratios:
    HDM   1.103
    HECM  0.971
    DD    0.432
  SOH alone therefore produces only a small amplification for HDM and does not
  explain the natural mid-life peak across all estimators.

  The selected 24 h C29 windows contain no qualifying 300 s CV event. Natural
  and disabled-reset results are consequently identical in those windows.

  Full-lifecycle results under the adverse -3% direction are:
                    Baseline MAE   Biased MAE   Delta-MAE
    DM                  0.083717     0.093948    0.010230
    HDM                 0.031535     0.038710    0.007175
    HECM                0.027332     0.031000    0.003667
    DD                  0.031570     0.036334    0.004764

  Bias contribution in the first versus last 10% of the lifecycle:
    DM      0.005682 -> 0.009537   change +0.003855
    HDM    -0.007644 -> 0.013053   change +0.020697
    HECM   -0.005668 -> 0.011347   change +0.017015
    DD      0.000454 -> 0.007562   change +0.007108
  The adverse current bias therefore becomes more damaging later in the C29
  life for every model, but the trajectory is not locally monotonic.

  Mean bias contribution from the first to last 20% of each interval:
    DM      0.007236 -> 0.008730   accumulation +0.001495
    HDM     0.006093 -> 0.007942   accumulation +0.001849
    HECM    0.007537 -> 0.006151   accumulation -0.001386
    DD      0.004009 -> 0.005303   accumulation +0.001294

  Mean bias contribution two hours before versus after full charge:
    DM      0.009632 -> 0.007734   correction -0.001898
    HDM     0.007630 -> 0.005046   correction -0.002584
    HECM    0.005565 -> 0.009763   response   +0.004199
    DD      0.004277 -> 0.001564   correction -0.002714
  Full charge therefore partially corrects DM, HDM, and DD. It is not a common
  hard reset for all architectures: HECM becomes temporarily worse directly
  after this operating point, then decreases across the next mean interval.

  The prior statement that both DD signs remain below baseline came from a
  reused pilot baseline rather than a fresh temporal baseline. It is superseded
  by the matched rerun above. Existing Figure 05d is superseded by Figure 05e.

INTERPRETATION
  A multiplicative current error integrates with signed charge history between
  correction events. DM, HDM, and DD show modest mean within-interval
  accumulation followed by partial full-charge correction. Across the complete
  cell life, all four estimators become more bias-sensitive in the final 10%,
  but repeated corrections and changing drive profiles prevent a monotonic
  point-by-point increase. HECM has the smallest full-life Delta-MAE and absolute
  biased MAE; it is the best overall estimator in this C29 mechanism test.

LIMITATIONS
  The controlled and continuous lifecycle tests use C29 as a mechanism study,
  not as six-cell population inference. The five-cell aging comparison excludes
  C27 because C27 has no frozen mid-life or aged window.
