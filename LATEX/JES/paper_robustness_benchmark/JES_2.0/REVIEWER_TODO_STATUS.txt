NON-NEGOTIABLE STUDY BOUNDARY
  The available dataset and LFP chemistry cannot be changed. JES2 therefore
  replaces the unsupported generalization claim with a six-cell LFP holdout
  validation and explicitly limits conclusions to this chemistry, dataset,
  temperature envelope, aging range, and duty-cycle family. NMC/NCA and pack-
  level transfer remain external-validity limitations, not simulated evidence.

REVIEWER 2 - EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

R2.1 Single-cell validation is insufficient
  Status: READY_FOR_RUN.
  Change: primary test set is C09, C13, C15, C25, C27, and C29. Quantitative
  characterization covers duration, SOH range, temperature range, current/C-rate,
  and EFC. Figure_16_Holdout_Cell_Coverage.png is generated by the JES2 result
  builder after the campaign. The dataset still cannot support cross-chemistry
  claims.
  Added test axes: fixed descriptive load groups (low C25/C27, middle
  C09/C13/C15, exploratory high C29) plus within-run SOH, instantaneous-load, temperature, and SOC
  states. Figure 20-Figure 23 and their CSV tables report these strata.

R2.2 Independence of model development and benchmark is unclear
  Status: DONE in software; PAPER_AFTER_RESULTS for manuscript wording.
  Change: SOC and SOH split equality is enforced before a campaign starts.
  Train: C01/C03/C05/C11/C17/C23. Validation: C07/C19/C21. Holdout:
  C09/C13/C15/C25/C27/C29. Non-holdout execution fails unless an explicit
  diagnostic override is supplied. Config/checkpoint/scaler paths and SHA256
  hashes are stored in each campaign manifest. Scalers were fitted on training
  cells only. Holdout cells must not be used for tuning, model selection, pruning,
  quantization, ECM identification, or figure/protocol tuning.
  Remaining evidence needed: document independently how the existing ECM lookup
  table was identified and confirm that no holdout cell entered its identification.
  Verification: short/prefixed cell identifiers are normalized consistently;
  C29 passed the holdout guard without a diagnostic override in a dry run.

R2.3 Initial SOC comparison is unfair because DD uses a Q_c proxy
  Status: DONE.
  Change: the same requested -10% SOC mismatch is mapped into each estimator's
  available online state. DM/HDM use their coulomb-counting SOC initialization,
  HECM its EKF SOC state, and DD the capacity-equivalent Q_c offset. Figure 07
  reports the realized initial estimator-output error before recovery time and
  excess-error AUC, so equivalence is checked quantitatively rather than assumed.
  The DD implementation route remains explicitly labelled in text and tables.

R2.4 Estimator-specific recovery bands bias recovery times
  Status: DONE.
  Change: common absolute SOC error threshold = 0.02, common continuous hold time
  = 300 s, and common censoring horizon = 24 h. Reported outputs include recovery
  time, censoring, initial post-event error, 1 h/6 h MAE, and excess-error AUC.
  Recovery plots use time-to-recovery-or-censoring and show the censored fraction
  separately, so non-recovered runs are not silently dropped from the comparison.

R2.5 Embedded deployment claims lack measured runtime/peak memory/CPU load
  Status: OPEN_HARDWARE.
  Software-paper action now: remove "embedded-ready" proof claims and describe
  flash/persistent-state estimates as analytical lower bounds only. Hardware phase
  must measure wall-clock latency distribution, peak runtime RAM including buffers
  and stack, flash image size, and CPU load on the named STM32 target. The common
  SOH service must be measured once and reported separately from each SOC core.

R2.6 Gaussian-noise robustness has no statistical validation
  Status: READY_FOR_RUN.
  Change: Gaussian sensor-noise cases use 10 deterministic seeds; random missing,
  jitter, and spike cases use 5. Aggregation first averages frozen SOH windows
  within each cell/seed and then weights the six cells equally. A
  hierarchical bootstrap over cells and nested seeds produces 95% confidence
  intervals. Raw per-run values remain available; per-second samples are not
  misrepresented as independent statistical replicates.
  Added inference: paired cell-level scenario effects and model differences,
  exact two-sided sign-flip tests, raw/standardized effect sizes, bootstrap CIs,
  and Holm-adjusted p-values. With only six independent cells, effect sizes and
  CIs remain primary; p-values must not be overinterpreted.

R2.7 Shared SOH estimator is a confounder
  Status: READY_FOR_RUN.
  Change: HDM, HECM, and DD receive one identical causal disturbed-input SOH trace
  per cell/scenario/seed. Primary results use LSTM SOH. A paired ideal reference-
  SOH ablation is run on baseline and the most relevant noise/bias/dropout cases.
  Figure_15_SOH_Ablation.png reports reference-minus-LSTM SOC error with paired CIs.

REVIEWER 3 - SCOPE, MECHANISMS, AND REPRODUCIBILITY

R3.1 Representativeness of one trajectory
  Status: READY_FOR_RUN.
  Change: six independent holdout cells span 1,074-3,648 h, minimum SOH 0.604-0.930,
  maximum temperature 31.9-49.4 degC, and distinct current/C-rate envelopes.
  Results will be reported per cell and as equal-weight cell macro statistics.
  The new load-class x SOH matrix makes profile/aging coverage explicit instead
  of collapsing every trajectory into one full-cell number.
  Runtime-safe protocol: 16 model-independent full-charge windows provide one
  representative 24 h segment per available cell/SOH state. The one-hour gap uses
  48 h. Selection uses measured operating features only and cannot optimize model
  rankings. C27 has no Mid-Life/Aged window and is reported as N/A there.

R3.2 Why LFP only; limits for NMC/NCA
  Status: PAPER_AFTER_RESULTS.
  Required wording: LFP was selected because the available controlled DoE aging
  dataset provides causal current/voltage/temperature/SOC/SOH trajectories and
  multiple independent cells. Flat LFP OCV behavior and chemistry-specific aging
  mean rankings must not be generalized to NMC/NCA without external validation.

R3.3 Shared SOH bottleneck may mask SOC-branch differences
  Status: READY_FOR_RUN. Same implementation as R2.7.

R3.4 Effect of hourly SOH cadence and fast degradation
  Status: READY_FOR_RUN plus explicit limitation.
  Change: the same hourly LSTM state can publish SOH every 1, 6, or 24 completed
  intervals. Figure_18_SOH_Cadence_Sensitivity.png compares the resulting SOC MAE.
  Limitation: the dataset has no physically observed abrupt capacity-loss event;
  no claim about fast degradation transients will be made from synthetic labels.

R3.5 DD Q_c proxy as real cold-start initialization
  Status: DONE in protocol; PAPER_AFTER_RESULTS for interpretation.
  Change: the DD Q_c initialization test is retained. Its requested SOC-equivalent
  offset, realized initial output mismatch, common-band recovery, and error AUC are
  reported alongside the other models, with the different state mapping disclosed.
  A separate hidden-state cold-start ablation remains optional future work.

R3.6 Physical mechanism of 27-29 h post-dropout recovery
  Status: READY_FOR_RUN.
  Change: each gap result records unobserved net charge, charge throughput,
  reference-SOC change, error immediately after data resume, common recovery time,
  censoring, and excess-error AUC. This distinguishes actual re-synchronization
  from an estimator-specific threshold artifact.

R3.7 Irregular sampling only +/-0.1 s
  Status: READY_FOR_RUN.
  Change: protocol contains +/-0.1 s, +/-0.5 s, and +/-0.9 s timing jitter, each
  repeated over 5 seeds and all available cell/SOH windows.

R3.8 Why DD is sensitive to voltage spikes
  Status: PARTIAL / PAPER_AFTER_RESULTS.
  Available mechanism hypothesis: DD directly consumes voltage and dU/dt, so a
  one-sample voltage spike enters both an absolute and derivative channel and can
  perturb the recurrent hidden state. JES2 reports spike delta-MAE and >5% output
  jumps across cells/seeds. Do not state this as causal proof unless an additional
  input-channel/hidden-state ablation confirms it.

R3.9 Risks of estimator-specific recovery thresholds
  Status: DONE. Replaced by the common criterion described under R2.4.

R3.10 Runtime memory estimates are lower bounds
  Status: OPEN_HARDWARE. Same action as R2.5.

R3.11 HECM current-bias result may depend on ECM lookup-table quality

R3.12 Temperature differences may originate from SOH LSTM

RESULT VALIDATION TODO (updated 2026-08-28)
  PARTIAL: The current-gain concern is resolved and independently rerun; the
  broader cross-scenario plausibility audit below remains open. The implemented
  and manuscript-declared protocol is a signed multiplicative sensor-gain error,
  I_meas=I(1+b), not a fixed additive current offset. Both signs were executed at
  0.5%, 1.5%, and 3.0%. Figure 05 reports the larger paired Delta-MAE for each
  cell and magnitude because one direction can compensate a directed baseline
  error. The opposite direction must not be interpreted as improved robustness.
  Focused 3% mechanism tests now include natural windows, a controlled identical-
  trace replay at SOH 0.962/0.897/0.831, natural versus disabled CV reset, and a
  continuous 1,344 h C29 lifecycle replay with 45 full-charge episodes. A fresh
  matched time-resolved rerun reports exact 5 min MAE for all 12 baseline/+3%/-3%
  entries. The adverse -3% direction increases full-life MAE by 0.010230 (DM),
  0.007175 (HDM), 0.003667 (HECM), and 0.004764 (DD). Bias contribution is larger
  in the last than first 10% of C29 life for all four models. DM, HDM, and DD show
  partial full-charge correction, so the increase is not point-by-point monotonic.
  HECM has the smallest absolute biased MAE (0.031000) and full-life Delta-MAE.
  Figure 05e reports the timeline, 44 normalized intervals, before/after response,
  and absolute ranking. This single-cell mechanism test is explanatory evidence,
  not independent-cell population proof.
  Apply the same plausibility review to every JES2 scenario before regenerating
  publication figures and tables: verify disturbance magnitude and direction,
  affected input channels, unchanged target/reference signals, identical frozen
  windows and seeds, baseline pairing, absolute metrics versus deltas, and the
  scale of the resulting errors. Investigate the currently very small errors
  and rerun tests where the disturbance is not visibly or numerically expressed.
  Record the final protocol decision, validation checks, rerun tag, and updated
  result manifest before marking this item DONE.
