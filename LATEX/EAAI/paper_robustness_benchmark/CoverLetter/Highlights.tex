\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Measurement-only benchmark for embedded battery state estimation.
    \item Direct, hybrid, and data-driven estimators are compared under one protocol.
    \item Disturbances cover bias, noise, missing data, timing faults, and spikes.
    \item Hybrid direct estimation is most accurate at baseline; hybrid ECM is most robust.
    \item Results support decision-oriented estimator selection for embedded BMS design.
\end{itemize}
